Frequently Asked Questions

How much can I reduce my image size?

Most users see a 60-80% reduction in file size when using our image compressor online to convert to WebP with our default settings.

Will I lose image quality?

Our algorithm allows you to compress image files using a "visually lossless" method, meaning the human eye usually cannot tell the difference between the original and the optimized version.

Are my images stored on your servers?

Never. As a privacy-focused free online image compressor, we use In-Memory Processing. Your images are processed in the system's temporary RAM and deleted automatically. We have no database and no permanent storage for user files.
