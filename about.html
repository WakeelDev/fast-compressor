About FastCompress

FastCompress was built with a simple mission: to make the web faster without compromising user privacy.

Many online tools store your images on their servers. We don't. As a free online image compressor, we prioritize your security. Our tool uses In-Memory Processing, meaning your files are optimized and then instantly cleared from the system.

Whether you are a blogger, developer, or social media manager, our goal is to help you compress image files, reduce page load times, and save storage space effortlessly.
